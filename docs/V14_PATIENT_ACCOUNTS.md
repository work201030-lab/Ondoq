# ONDOQ V14 — Global Patient Accounts

- Global patient registration/login by phone + password.
- Appointment history across clinics matched by phone.
- Completed appointments can be rated from the authenticated patient account.
- Clinic discovery and public booking remain available without an account.
- Patient account deletion endpoint: `DELETE /api/patient-auth/account`.
- Public patient deletion information: `public/legal.html#delete`.
